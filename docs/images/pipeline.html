Why judge pictures first

A picture try takes seconds; a shape and its paint take minutes. Most mistakes are already visible in the picture (a shadow, the wrong thing, letters), so they are refused before any shape is built.

Retries are bounded

At most 8 picture tries, 2 shapes and 3 paintings of the six views. The touch-up paints a foreign fill again from more seeds, then leaves what is still foreign out. Nothing loops without end, and every try is kept in the report.

A failure is still shown

An object that fails a check is written with its report, marked failing, so you can see what went wrong. Only a prompt that is refused, or has no picture that passes, makes nothing.
