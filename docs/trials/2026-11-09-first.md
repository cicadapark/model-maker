# The first trial (9 November 2026): what failed, and what was decided

Sixteen prompts (`tools/trial/trial-prompts.txt`) made on an RTX 3080 laptop card: 6 passed every
check. The finish was run again on a CPU, with nothing painted again, after the changes below: 8 pass,
and two more (the jug, the book) fail only on their picture.

## Decisions

Each failure was looked at by eye in its report. Where the model was wrong, the step is fixed; where
the check misread it, the measure is fixed. No limit was loosened.

1. **Holes and torn edges** (chair, barrel, stool, jug, candle). Every hole was a seam of corners at
   the same place never joined (4 open edges each); every torn edge a doubled triangle, a fold the
   cut to 60,000 triangles flattened. *Step fixed:* the standard clean-up before every check
   (`mesh_repair.py`). After joining, one hole was left in all sixteen: the barrel's, 8 edges and
   22.8 mm round (2.5 % of its size). Holes up to 24 edges and 5 % of the size round are filled; a
   lost chair leg (some 13 % round) would not be. "No holes" stays at 0.
2. **The chair's four loose pieces, the lantern's one** were hollows sealed inside the main piece
   (wound inward, negative volume): one in each chair leg, a 129 cm³ chamber in the lantern, 14 %
   of its surface, which no view can paint. *Step fixed:* hollows are dropped in the clean-up. The
   barrel's hoops, split from the staves they touch, showed that "one main piece" must count
   touching pieces as one. *Measure fixed:* corners at the same place are joined before counting.
3. **The chair settles 3.6° back.** Truly wrong: both shape seeds made its back legs 3 cm short.
   Its picture was painted from the front at eye level (asked "slightly from above"), so the far
   legs ended higher in it. *Step fixed:* objects are asked for from about thirty degrees above
   (to be measured on the card).
4. **The round table's outline (69 %).** Partly wrong: the model has a thin web between its legs
   under the top. Partly the measure being hard on thin legs painted in perspective. An overlap with
   a tolerance of 1 % of the height was tried and rejected: it reads 100 % for every thin object,
   the wrong longbow too. *Kept as it is*; the table is made again with four shape seeds on the card.
5. **The chest's and shield's pictures** (and, once the shadow measure was corrected, the book's,
   the jug's, the table's): not clean. Each object is painted floating, with a floor meeting a wall
   and its shadow cast on that floor well below it. The floor check was right; the shadow check had
   searched only a band just under the object. *Measure fixed:* the search reaches the picture's
   foot, each height against the far backdrop at the same height. A shadow away from the object does
   not reach the cut-out, but it shows light from one side, which stays in the object's colours.
   Across the trial 37 of 76 picture tries passed before and 26 after. *Step to fix:* our own lead
   words say "floating in empty space"; two other leads are measured against it on the card.
6. **Paint coverage** (jug 83 %, candle 82 %, lantern 70 %). The jug's unpainted texels were mostly
   inside its neck, seen only looking into its mouth; the candle's in the narrow gap round it in its
   dish. *Measure fixed:* each texel counts by how often it is in view (32 directions); the plain
   share is shown beside it. Jug 95.8 %, candle 91.6 %. The lantern, its hollow gone, is at 89.96 %:
   the inner sides of its cage bars and the undersides of its rims are truly unpainted (seen only at
   a slant by the six views), so it **stays a fail**; the paint step's views are where to fix it.
7. **The longbow.** Truly wrong, but not for thinness: painted edge-on, its bend was out of sight and
   the shape model made it wave (a W, 39° kink). *Step fixed:* a bow is painted facing the viewer.
   Of the two follow-ups planned, the string is built: a bow that comes out as one curve gets a
   string drawn between its tips by our own code, outside the checks and marked in the sidecar; a
   straight or waving bow gets none (the trial's waving one gets none). Rebuilding a shaft from its
   line was not needed: every shaft and haft in the trial came out straight and whole.

## Paint time

The paint is 7.8 to 9.2 minutes of each object's 10.5 to 14.8 (MV-Adapter at 50 steps). Likeness is
now measured (the painted object from its picture's view, the mean colour difference) to compare
30 and 20 steps with 50 on the same shapes, with the coverage beside it.
