# docs

How the pipeline works, with its numbers and what we learned making it.

* `setup.md`: setting a PC up with `setup.sh`, what it installs and checks, a 12 GB card, the times on a clean PC.
* `objects.md`: 3-D objects from a prompt, every check and its limit, the marks, thin parts.
* `metrics.md`: the job ledger, what each line holds, and the summary made from it.
* `trials/`: the notes of the release's first two trials: what failed, why, and what was decided (the later trials
  are described in `objects.md`; their notes are not published).
